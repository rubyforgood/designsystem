# Spacing and elevation

## Spacing

All spacing comes from one 4px scale. Use the scale for padding, gaps and margins rather than
picking values by eye, so related layouts line up.

<SpacingScale />

## Radius

Three radii: one for surfaces such as cards and modals, one for controls such as buttons and
inputs, and a full round for pills and avatars.

<TokenSamples prefix="--radius-" kind="radius" />

## Elevation

Two levels. Surfaces such as cards sit just above the page. Overlays such as menus, popovers
and modals float above everything else.
